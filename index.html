About Us

At Custom Flatpacks Adelaide, we believe your kitchen and cabinetry should be designed around you — not the other way around. We are a family-owned Adelaide business passionate about creating practical, stylish and personalised flatpack kitchens and cabinetry that suit your home, your lifestyle and your budget. Unlike the big-box approach, we pride ourselves on providing a personalised, local service from start to finish. We take the time to understand what you need, help you make the right choices and work with you to create a finished result you’ll be proud of. Whether you're renovating your kitchen, updating your cabinetry or creating a completely new space, we can help bring your ideas to life with custom solutions, personal service and attention to detail.
